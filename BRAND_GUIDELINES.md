# Revlio Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence
**Mission**: "Powering Digital Expansion"
**Tone**: Futuristic, Premium, Confident, Global.
**Style**: Dark-mode, Neon, Glassmorphism, 3D Tech.

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Deep Navy (Background)**: `#050510`
- **Pure White (Text)**: `#FFFFFF`

### Accent Colors
- **Electric Cyan**: `#00F0FF` (Growth, Energy, Tech)
- **Royal Purple**: `#7000FF` (Creativity, Wisdom, Premium)

### Gradients
- **Neon Flow**: `linear-gradient(45deg, #00F0FF, #7000FF)`
- **Glass Overlay**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`

## 3. Typography

### Primary Font: **Outfit**
Used for Headings and Body. A geometric sans-serif that feels modern and approachable.
- **Headings**: Bold (700) or Extra Bold (800). All caps for major statements.
- **Body**: Light (300) or Regular (400) for readability.

## 4. Logo Usage
- **Primary**: 3D Text Logo with Gradient Symbol.
- **Clear Space**: Always maintain 20px padding minimum around the logo.
- **Placement**: Top-left on web; Centered on decks/end-screens.
- **Effects**: Subtle outer glow (`drop-shadow(0 0 10px cyan)`) is permitted in dark mode.

## 5. UI & Interaction
- **Cards**: Glassmorphism with thin white borders and blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`).
- **Hover States**: Elements should "lift" (`transform: translateY(-5px)`) and "glow".
- **Scroll**: Parallax effects to create depth.
