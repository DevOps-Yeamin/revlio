# Revlio Asset Generation Prompts

Use these prompts in AI tools (Midjourney, Runway Gen-2, ChatGPT) to create consistent brand assets.

## 1. Website Hero Visual (Midjourney / DALL-E)
> **Prompt**: Create a premium 3D website hero section visual for a modern digital agency named 'Revlio'. Scene style: dark-mode, ultra-modern, cinematic tech aesthetic. Abstract data streams, expanding grids, and light trails appearing to flow forward, symbolizing growth and scalability. Visually represent Lead Generation and Business Solutions through subtle 3D UI panels and holographic dashboards. Deep blue, electric purple, cyan highlights.

## 2. Pitch Deck Cover (Midjourney / Firefly)
> **Prompt**: A futuristic corporate pitch deck cover background. Dark navy blue backdrop with soft neon purple and cyan abstract geometric lines rising upwards to symbolize growth. Minimalist, clean, high-end business aesthetic. Glassmorphism texture overlays. 8k resolution, aspect ratio 16:9.

## 3. Intro Video Scene (Runway Gen-2 / Pika)
> **Prompt**: Cinematic 3D animation of a glowing digital network expanding across a dark globe. The camera flies through the network nodes, showing connections lighting up in electric cyan. The style is high-tech, fast-paced, and professional. 4k, smooth motion blur.

## 4. Social Media Backgrounds
> **Prompt**: Abstract 3D background texture, dark mode, blurred neon lights in purple and blue, cybernetic grid patterns, soft bokeh, suitable for text overlay.

## 5. Team/Office Concept
> **Prompt**: A futuristic digital agency office space, diverse team collaborating at holographic workstations, diverse professionals, warm lighting, modern glass architecture, view of a digital city skyline at night.
